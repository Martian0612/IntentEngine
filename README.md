# IntentEngine 📖

> A semantic analytics tool that transforms raw YouTube activity into an actionable behavioral mirror.

`intentengine` analyzes your YouTube watch history, search queries, comments, and playlists using Gemini. Instead of relying on rigid keyword filters or simple time-spent charts, it evaluates multi-dimensional relationships across your activity data to help you detect passive consumption traps, break information-hoarding loops, and align your viewing habits with your actual goals.

---

## The Problem

Modern content platforms are engineered for infinite retention. As a result, users frequently fall into subconscious behavioral patterns:

* **Aspirational Hoarding ("Bookmark Graveyard"):** Saving tutorials or educational videos to playlists that sit unwatched for months.
* **Lead-Magnet Loops:** Commenting on short-form videos to request resources, creating a backlog of reference material that is never reviewed or applied.
* **Algorithmic Drift:** Intending to research a specific subject, but gradually getting pulled into reactive entertainment or surface-level commentary.

Static analytics and keyword filters miss these patterns because they only evaluate isolated video titles. `intentengine` focuses on **semantic intent and relational patterns**—cross-referencing what you save, search, watch, and comment on to reveal how you actually engage with content over time.

---

## Core Principles

* **Domain-Agnostic Design:** Built to analyze behavioral patterns across any field—whether you use YouTube for software engineering, academic research, creative trades, fitness, or self-directed learning.
* **Semantic Analysis over Static Rules:** Leverages LLM reasoning to evaluate actual transcript content alongside multi-stream activity data, identifying true context rather than relying on clickbait titles.
* **Relational Insights:** Cross-references disconnected activity streams (watches vs. comments vs. playlists) to highlight the gap between intent and action.
